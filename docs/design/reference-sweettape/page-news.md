## News card anatomy

- Portrait card, radius about 42px, cream fill.
- Top: small centred category label (NEWS or EVENT) in muted green over a dotted rule.
- Title in Inter Tight bold about 24px, sentence case, up to three lines.
- Round lime arrow button under the title.
- Photo bleeds off the bottom-right corner, with a kraft tape strip across its top.
- Bottom-left: big date numeral in condensed type, month and year beneath.

## Behaviour

- Filter labels switch the card set; non-matching cards fade out.
- Cards fade up on enter, staggered.
- Highlight is the newest or pinned post and links to its article.
