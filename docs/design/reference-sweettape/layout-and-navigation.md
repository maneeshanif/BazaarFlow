# Layout and navigation

## Skeleton (all pages)

1. Skip-to-content link
2. `NAV`, absolute at the top, transparent, 93px tall: HOME, ABOUT, PRODUCTS ("Our family" in the footer), NEWS, CONTACT. Links are condensed uppercase ink green on lime. In the DOM the nav labels are split per letter.
3. `MAIN`: stacked full-width sections with curved joins
4. `FOOTER`: forest green, 1,025px tall

Routes in nav order: `/`, `/about`, `/products`, `/news`, `/contact`. The footer calls Products "Our family".

## Fixed overlays

| Element | Position | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pull-me ribbon | top right | Playful interaction toy (drag to unroll) |
| Brand badge | top right, below ribbon | Small dark rounded tile with a lime asterisk logo |
| Audio pill | bottom right | Sound on/off |

## Footer

- Four nav labels spread across the full width (OUR FAMILY, ABOUT, NEWS, CONTACT), lime, each with a dot under it.
- Giant two-line lime wordmark "STICK BY YOU" centred, tilted, with the logo badge on top.
- Five tape rolls arranged around the wordmark (red bottom-left, indigo mid-left, orange mid-right, sky bottom-right).
- Round lime social buttons: Instagram (left), Facebook and TikTok (right).
- Copyright line at the very bottom: "Copyright 2026" plus the company name.

## Intro transition

On every first load and route change a lime screen shows the centred logo badge, then coloured arcs (coral, orange, yellow, lime, sky, indigo) sweep up from the bottom and reveal the page. Take this as the signature moment.
