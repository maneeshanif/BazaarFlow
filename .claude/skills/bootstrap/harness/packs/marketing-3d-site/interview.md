# Marketing site, cinematic and 3D-forward — technical interview

Ask only what changes the build. Each question carries a recommendation and a free-tier default; the user answers yes or names a change.

## Q1. Which motion level?

- **Recommend:** Cinematic 3D for a brand or marketing site: animated and scroll-driven from the first build. Refined if the audience is on weak devices.
- **Free default:** Motion is code and assets only; no paid tool is required.

## Q2. How is the 3D made: real-time WebGL (Three.js) or pre-rendered video / frame sequence scrubbed by scroll?

- **Recommend:** Pre-rendered frames or video for the hero when the budget or device range is tight (predictable cost, great look); real-time WebGL where interaction with the 3D object is the point.
- **Free default:** Frame sequence from free or low-cost tools; Three.js is free.

## Q3. Who produces the 3D, video and photography?

- **Recommend:** Decide before design: stock, generated, commissioned or client-supplied. The schedule depends on it.
- **Free default:** Optimised stock or generated assets first; replace later if the brand needs it.

## Q4. How many pages and sections, and what is the one action every page drives?

- **Recommend:** Few pages, each with one primary action; long pages with a clear story arc.
- **Free default:** None needed.

## Q5. Does the content change without a developer?

- **Recommend:** Files or MDX in the repo for the MVP; add a CMS only when someone non-technical must edit weekly.
- **Free default:** No CMS cost until the trigger.

## Q6. Contact or quote forms: where do submissions go?

- **Recommend:** Server action to a transactional email service with spam protection; store nothing you do not need.
- **Free default:** Free tier of an email service.

## Q7. Which sites does the client admire?

- **Recommend:** Up to three, for the inspiration research (default source: the GSAP Showcase).
- **Free default:** None needed.
